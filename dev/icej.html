Overview & Purpose

This project aims to gather the entire catalog of the musician João Antunes, under the stage name Ice J, including albums, singles, EPs, live performances, and more. The website also contains live show dates, music videos, and a brief introduction to the musician behind the project. It also includes a contact form and clickable icons linking to social media and streaming platforms. All of this is integrated into a professional and minimalist design.
