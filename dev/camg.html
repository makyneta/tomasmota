Overview & Purpose

This project aims to increase the digital reach of the Marinha Grande Athletics Club. The website has three sections: the club section, with information about the club, social bodies, national champions, statistics and newsletter; the membership section, followed by the regulations; and the competitions offered and produced by the club, followed by news, gallery and contacts. All development was carried out in conjunction with members of the club's management, including the president and vice-president in their respective positions at the time of creation. The website also includes legal information such as terms and conditions, privacy policy, cookie policy and complaint book, as well as a site map.
