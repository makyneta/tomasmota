// about the founder

TOMÁS MOTA

Tomás Mota is a full-stack developer and photographer based in Leiria, Portugal. Since 2024, he has worked with theatre companies, sports organisations and educational groups, building practical solutions for the people who use them.

His work spans web development, photography, design and video, as well as IT support and live production. He is used to working across both the technical detail of a project and the practical demands of delivering it.

Through Makyneta, he brings these skills together to help organisations turn ideas into reliable, well-crafted work.
