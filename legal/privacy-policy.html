01 —

Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Tomás Mota, operating under the name Makyneta, collects, uses and protects personal data in accordance with the Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation — GDPR) and Law no. 58/2019 of 8 August (the Portuguese data protection law implementing the GDPR).

Data controller: Tomás Mota (Makyneta), Leiria, Portugal.
Contact: tomas@makyneta.dev

02 —

Data Collected

Depending on how you interact with Makyneta, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact data — name, email address and telephone number voluntarily provided through contact forms or direct communication
  • Project data — information you share as part of a service engagement (briefs, references, feedback)
  • Newsletter data — name and email address if you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Usage data — anonymised browsing data, such as pages visited, browser type and device type, collected through analytics tools
  • Photography — images in which you may appear, taken at events or in public settings where Makyneta has been contracted

We do not collect sensitive personal data (as defined in Article 9 of the GDPR) unless strictly necessary and with your explicit consent.

03 —

Purpose of Collection

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing requested services
  • Establishing contractual relationships and delivering project work
  • Sending newsletters or updates if you have subscribed
  • Improving the website and user experience through anonymised analytics
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations in Portugal and the EU

We do not sell, rent or market your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

04 —

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR (Article 6), we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for newsletter subscriptions and non-essential cookies
  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — when processing is necessary to fulfil a service agreement
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — for website analytics and commercial communications
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) — when required by Portuguese or EU law
05 —

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law:

  • Contact and project data: retained for the duration of the project plus up to 5 years, in accordance with Portuguese commercial law (Article 40 of the Commercial Code)
  • Newsletter data: retained until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: retained in anonymised form for up to 26 months

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or permanently anonymised.

06 —

Third Parties and Data Transfers

We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers strictly for the purpose of operating our services:

  • Formspree — contact form processing (USA; Standard Contractual Clauses apply)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (USA; GDPR compliance through SCCs)
  • Analytics providers — anonymised usage monitoring only

When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place as required by Chapter V of the GDPR.

We do not transfer your data to any other third parties without your explicit consent.

07 —

Your Rights

Under the GDPR and Portuguese law (Law no. 58/2019), you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access (Article 15) — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Article 16) — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (Article 17) — request deletion of your data (the "right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction (Article 18) — request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability (Article 20) — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object (Article 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tomas@makyneta.dev. We will respond within 30 days, as required by Article 12 of the GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (National Data Protection Commission): www.cnpd.pt.

08 —

Cookies

This website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience and analyse usage patterns.

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function; they do not require consent
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors interact with the site; they require consent

In accordance with the Electronic Communications Law (Law no. 41/2004 of 18 August, as amended by Law no. 46/2012), non-essential cookies are only placed with your prior consent.

This website does not currently use third-party advertising cookies.
09 —

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure — in accordance with Article 32 of the GDPR.

These measures include encrypted communications (HTTPS), access controls and the use of privacy-respecting service providers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
